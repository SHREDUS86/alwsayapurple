OUR SERVICES

AL WSAYA provides diversified services across construction, logistics, manpower support, procurement, and technical operations.

Construction (EPC)

Execution of construction works including buildings, steel structures, HVAC systems, power systems, fencing, utilities, drainage, and turnkey camp facilities.

Logistics

Transportation, equipment scheduling, asset management, operator support, and logistics planning for complex contracts and field operations.

Manpower

Provision of skilled local and international workforce, recruitment services, visa processing, transportation, accommodation, and staff support services.

General Supplies

Fast and competitive procurement for local and imported materials through strategic partnerships in Iraq, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and the UK.

Operations & Maintenance

Supervised O&M solutions with qualified technicians covering electrical, plumbing, HVAC, welding, carpentry, maintenance planning, and site support.

Health & Safety (HSE)

A compliance-driven work culture focused on safe delivery, risk reduction, accountability, and implementation of strong HSE procedures across all projects.

ABOUT US

Founded in 2007, AL WSAYA has developed into a structured, professional, and organized company serving government, NGO, and private sector clients throughout Iraq.

Built on Iraqi Ownership, Driven by Professional Standards

AL WSAYA began its journey through subcontracting and steadily earned the trust of international organizations, coalition-related operations, Iraqi government entities, and other partners through reliable service and strong execution.

Over the years, the company expanded its internal divisions, reinvested in its people, and strengthened its ability to deliver projects in construction, logistics, procurement, and technical operations.

  • Founded in 2007 and operating across Iraq
  • 100% Iraqi-owned with modern management practices
  • Experienced in public sector, NGO, and international project support
  • Focused on quality, safety, compliance, and accountability
